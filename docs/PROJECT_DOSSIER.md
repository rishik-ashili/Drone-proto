## 1. Summary

**What exists.** A software-in-the-loop digital twin of the drone, together with the companion
software that will fly the real one. Specifically:

- **Simulation:** quadrotor physics, IMU, optical flow, rangefinder, barometer, magnetometer and
  depth sensors, an EKF2-style estimator, a PX4-style controller with failsafes, and a rendered
  downward camera.
- **Companion software:**
  - perception (custom YOLOv8n + ArUco)
  - landmark localizer
  - mission state machine
  - obstacle avoidance and geofence
  - health monitor and failsafe chain
  - teach-and-repeat floor-map matching
  - stereo-VIO correction
  - pilot Position mode and click-to-go
- **Vehicle backends:** the companion software runs unchanged against three: the built-in
  simulator, PX4 through MAVSDK (SITL or a Pixhawk), and PX4 through ROS 2 + uXRCE-DDS (Jetson).
- **Tooling:**
  - live browser dashboard, logs, reports, replay and video
  - synthetic dataset generator, training and model card
  - benchmark against the plan's exit criteria
  - Gazebo world and model export
  - PX4 parameter file
  - calibration, printables, bench check, ULog analysis and deployment

**Numbers** (simulator, [benchmark_results.md](benchmark_results.md)):

| Measure | Result |
|---|---|
| Mean waypoint error, flow only | 0.57 m |

## 3. Architecture and the 20 Hz loop

The layers follow plan section 3 ([ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md), [diagram](architecture.svg)):

| Layer | Simulator | Real drone |
|---|---|---|
| L1 flight control + estimation | [`FlightController`](../gdnav/sim/flight_controller.py#L43), [`PositionEstimator`](../gdnav/sim/estimator.py#L26) | PX4 v1.17 on the Pixhawk 6C, [`config/px4/gps_denied_flow.params`](../config/px4/gps_denied_flow.params) |
| L2 perception | [`CvNode`](../gdnav/perception/cv_node.py#L49), [`MapMatcher`](../gdnav/perception/map_matcher.py#L137) | same |
| L3 navigation | [`MissionStateMachine`](../gdnav/navigation/state_machine.py#L69) | same |
| L4 interface | [`SimVehicle`](../gdnav/sim/sim_vehicle.py#L36) | [`Px4MavsdkVehicle`](../gdnav/interface/px4_mavsdk.py#L48), [`Px4RosVehicle`](../jetson/ros2/gdnav_ros/gdnav_ros/px4_ros_vehicle.py#L36) |
| L5 safety | [`HealthMonitor`](../gdnav/safety/health_monitor.py#L25), [`Geofence`](../gdnav/navigation/geofence.py#L7) | same + PX4 failsafes |
| L6 tooling | dashboard, logs, benchmark, training | same + PX4 SITL, QGC |

All three backends implement [`VehicleInterface`](../gdnav/interface/vehicle.py#L51), and the loop is
[`MissionRunner.tick`](../gdnav/runner.py#L178). One tick, in order:

1. **Advance the simulator** by 50 ms (simulator only): [`SimVehicle.advance`](../gdnav/sim/sim_vehicle.py#L108)
   → [`SimVehicle._physics_step`](../gdnav/sim/sim_vehicle.py#L116) at 200 Hz. This runs flow and range at
   50 Hz, the camera at 15 Hz, VIO at 30 Hz and the depth scan at 10 Hz.
2. **Fault schedule.** Inject or clear faults: [`MissionRunner.inject`](../gdnav/runner.py#L154).
3. **Telemetry:** [`VehicleInterface.telemetry`](../gdnav/interface/vehicle.py#L58).
4. **VIO** (if `nav_source: vio`). Correct each sample and stream it as external vision:
   [`MissionRunner._forward_vio`](../gdnav/runner.py#L254).
5. **Perception** on a new frame: [`CvNode.process`](../gdnav/perception/cv_node.py#L98). Each confirmed
   landmark becomes a fix through [`MissionRunner._send_fix`](../gdnav/runner.py#L270), latency-compensated
   using the estimate at exposure time.
6. **Floor map** (if enabled): teach or match at 4 Hz with
   [`MissionRunner._floor_map_step`](../gdnav/runner.py#L276).
7. **Safety:** [`HealthMonitor.evaluate`](../gdnav/safety/health_monitor.py#L43), plus VIO tracking through
   [`HealthMonitor.report`](../gdnav/safety/health_monitor.py#L85).
8. **Navigation:** [`MissionStateMachine.step`](../gdnav/navigation/state_machine.py#L256) streams a setpoint.
9. **Bookkeeping:** metrics ([`MissionRunner._metrics`](../gdnav/runner.py#L313)), the JSONL log
   ([`MissionRunner._log`](../gdnav/runner.py#L332)), and the dashboard snapshot
   ([`MissionRunner.snapshot`](../gdnav/runner.py#L418)).

Frames and conventions: [`gdnav/common/geometry.py`](../gdnav/common/geometry.py). The world frame is local NED in metres from
takeoff, and the body frame is FRD. The downward camera's image top is the nose
([`R_CAM_FROM_BODY`](../gdnav/common/geometry.py#L44)). Timing always uses
[`VehicleInterface.now`](../gdnav/interface/vehicle.py#L53).

## 5. Subsystems in depth

### 5.1 World, floor texture and rendered camera

- **World:** [`World`](../gdnav/sim/world.py#L64) loads [`worlds/indoor_arena.yaml`](../worlds/indoor_arena.yaml) (20 × 20 m, 7 landmarks,
  3 pillars, 18 distractors) or [`worlds/plan_arena.yaml`](../worlds/plan_arena.yaml) (the plan's 3 m example).
- **Floor:** [`World.build_texture`](../gdnav/sim/world.py#L140) paints a 1 cm/px texture:
  [`floor_texture`](../gdnav/sim/world.py#L195) (multi-scale noise and speckles), the measurement grid,
  distractors ([`random_distractor`](../gdnav/sim/world.py#L261)), obstacle footprints and landmarks
  ([`render_landmark`](../gdnav/sim/world.py#L222): helipad, red pad, blue pad, yellow cross, ArUco).
  [`paste`](../gdnav/sim/world.py#L285) places them with yaw.
- **Camera:** [`DownwardCamera.render`](../gdnav/sim/camera.py#L47) warps the texture with
  [`DownwardCamera.homography`](../gdnav/sim/camera.py#L35) (K·R_cb·R_bw·M). It adds motion blur from body
  velocity, vignetting, light level and noise. 640×480, 80° HFOV, 15 Hz.
- **Classes:** the YOLO class list is [`YOLO_CLASSES`](../gdnav/sim/world.py#L20).
- **Left:** lighting is not physically modelled. Gazebo ([`export`](../scripts/export_gazebo.py#L158)) and
  real images will be harder.

### 5.2 Vehicle dynamics and sensors

- **Airframe:** [`Quadrotor.step`](../gdnav/sim/quadrotor.py#L79) models a 1.6 kg 450 mm quad: thrust along
  body −z, first-order roll/pitch response (τ = 0.07 s), linear drag against the wind, ground contact
  and distance flown. [`Battery`](../gdnav/sim/quadrotor.py#L18) is a 4S 5 Ah pack with sag.
- **Sensors:** [`SensorSuite`](../gdnav/sim/sensors.py#L34).

| Sensor | Model | Function |
|---|---|---|
| IMU | Noise + bias random walk | [`SensorSuite.imu`](../gdnav/sim/sensors.py#L51) |
| Optical flow | ±3 % scale error, height-dependent noise, quality drop on featureless floors | [`SensorSuite.flow`](../gdnav/sim/sensors.py#L59) |
| Rangefinder, barometer | Range noise; barometer drift | [`SensorSuite.range`](../gdnav/sim/sensors.py#L78), [`SensorSuite.baro`](../gdnav/sim/sensors.py#L85) |
| Magnetometer | ±3.5° bias, the main dead-reckoning error | [`SensorSuite.mag_heading`](../gdnav/sim/sensors.py#L91) |
| Depth fan | 21 rays over 100°, ray-cast to the pillars | [`SensorSuite.depth_scan`](../gdnav/sim/sensors.py#L96) |
| Stereo VIO | Distance-scaled drift, heading drift, tracking loss with a reset counter | [`VioSensor`](../gdnav/sim/vio.py#L36) |

- **Collisions:** [`SimVehicle._check_collisions`](../gdnav/sim/sim_vehicle.py#L176) records ground-truth contacts.
- **Left:** replace the noise numbers in `config/sim.yaml` with values measured from bench logs
  ([`analyse`](../scripts/analyze_ulog.py#L52)).

### 5.3 Estimator (EKF2 analogue)

[`PositionEstimator`](../gdnav/sim/estimator.py#L26) runs three linear Kalman filters: horizontal
[px, py, vx, vy, bax, bay], vertical [pz, vz, baz], and heading [yaw, gyro bias].

| Step | Function | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Predict | [`PositionEstimator.predict`](../gdnav/sim/estimator.py#L80) | IMU specific force rotated to the world, minus bias |
| Flow | [`PositionEstimator.update_flow`](../gdnav/sim/estimator.py#L126) | Fuses body velocity; χ²-gated |
| Height | [`PositionEstimator.update_range`](../gdnav/sim/estimator.py#L140), [`PositionEstimator.update_baro`](../gdnav/sim/estimator.py#L146) | Rangefinder and barometer |
| Heading | [`PositionEstimator.update_heading`](../gdnav/sim/estimator.py#L151) | Magnetometer or ArUco |
| External vision | [`PositionEstimator.update_external_vision`](../gdnav/sim/estimator.py#L181), [`PositionEstimator.update_ev_velocity`](../gdnav/sim/estimator.py#L172), [`PositionEstimator.reset_horizontal`](../gdnav/sim/estimator.py#L163) | Landmark fixes / VIO; resets on reset_counter |
| Accuracy | [`PositionEstimator.pos_std`](../gdnav/sim/estimator.py#L70) | Plays the role of PX4's eph |

[`SimVehicle`](../gdnav/sim/sim_vehicle.py#L36) runs a second, dead-reckoning-only estimator (`self.dr`) on
the same data, for the "without AI" comparison.

**Test:** [`test_external_vision_gate_rejects_outlier`](../tests/test_estimator.py#L7).

**Left:** this is not PX4's EKF2. Heading and position are decoupled, so position fixes don't
correct heading. Validate against PX4 SITL.

### 5.4 Flight controller (PX4 analogue)

[`FlightController.update`](../gdnav/sim/flight_controller.py#L130) implements:

- **Modes:** DISARMED, ARMED, OFFBOARD, HOLD, LAND and POSCTL ([`FlightMode`](../gdnav/sim/flight_controller.py#L33)).
- **Control cascade:** position P → velocity PID → acceleration → thrust and tilt, all using the
  *estimated* state.
- **Offboard rules:** Offboard is refused unless setpoints stream
  ([`FlightController.start_offboard`](../gdnav/sim/flight_controller.py#L90)). An offboard-loss failsafe
  fires after `COM_OF_LOSS_T` (Hold or Land per `COM_OBL_RC_ACT`).
- **Battery:** critical battery → Land.
- **Position mode:** [`FlightController.position_mode`](../gdnav/sim/flight_controller.py#L107). Sticks set
  velocity; with sticks centred it brakes, then holds position and altitude. It auto-disarms after
  2 s on the ground.
- **Collision prevention:** [`FlightController._collision_prevention`](../gdnav/sim/flight_controller.py#L236)
  (`CP_DIST`, 1 m).

On the real drone, PX4 itself does all of this, configured by [`config/px4/gps_denied_flow.params`](../config/px4/gps_denied_flow.params).

### 5.5 Perception pipeline

[`CvNode.process`](../gdnav/perception/cv_node.py#L98), per frame:

1. **Undistort** if calibrated ([`CvNode._load_calibration`](../gdnav/perception/cv_node.py#L69)).
2. **Detect.** ArUco ([`ArucoDetector`](../gdnav/perception/aruco_detector.py#L10)) runs, plus the YOLO
   model ([`YoloDetector`](../gdnav/perception/yolo_detector.py#L20): letterbox to 320, ONNX Runtime with
   TensorRT/CUDA/CPU, NMS). The colour fallback is [`ColorDetector`](../gdnav/perception/color_detector.py#L21).
   A decoded ArUco tag supersedes YOLO's `aruco_tag` box.
3. **Localise and associate:** [`CvNode._observe`](../gdnav/perception/cv_node.py#L147) →
   [`LandmarkLocalizer.pixel_to_offset`](../gdnav/perception/landmark_localizer.py#L28), then association by
   tag id, or by class with the nearest landmark within 2.5 m. The fix uncertainty grows with height
   and off-centre angle. ArUco tags also give heading
   ([`LandmarkLocalizer.marker_yaw`](../gdnav/perception/landmark_localizer.py#L37)).
4. **Temporal filter:** [`TemporalFilter.update`](../gdnav/perception/temporal_filter.py#L17) (3 frames).
5. **Annotate** for the dashboard: [`CvNode.annotate`](../gdnav/perception/cv_node.py#L181).

Detection types: [`Detection`](../gdnav/perception/detections.py#L9),
[`LandmarkObservation`](../gdnav/perception/detections.py#L26). Fault injection
(`false_landmark`) uses [`CvNode._fake_detection`](../gdnav/perception/cv_node.py#L174).

**Tests:** all of [`tests/test_perception.py`](../tests/test_perception.py).

**Left:** real images and lens calibration.

### 5.6 Drift correction sources

| Source | Code | How it reaches the estimator |
|---|---|---|
| Landmark fixes | [`MissionRunner._send_fix`](../gdnav/runner.py#L270) | Flow mode: `send_vision_position`. VIO mode: [`MissionRunner._fuse_fix_with_vio`](../gdnav/runner.py#L301) |
| Stereo VIO | [`MissionRunner._forward_vio`](../gdnav/runner.py#L254) | [`LandmarkVioFusion`](../gdnav/bridge/landmark_vio_fusion.py#L28) corrects the VIO frame, then `send_odometry` (pos + vel + yaw, reset counter) |
| Floor map | [`MissionRunner._floor_map_step`](../gdnav/runner.py#L276) | [`MapMatcher.match`](../gdnav/perception/map_matcher.py#L180) → fix with map uncertainty (`map_std_m`) → `_send_fix` |

**Fusion maths.** The state is (dx, dy, dyaw) mapping the VIO frame to the map frame. Process noise
grows with distance flown, and the measurement Jacobian includes the rotation lever arm. When VIO
resets, the correction is re-anchored to the flight controller's estimate, which flew on optical
flow during the outage. Large corrections bump the external-vision reset counter.

**Map matching.**
- Orthorectify the frame at the estimated pose
  ([`MapMatcher.orthorectify`](../gdnav/perception/map_matcher.py#L153), [`ground_to_image_h`](../gdnav/perception/map_matcher.py#L30)).
- Enhance contrast (CLAHE), match ORB features (ratio test), and fit a RANSAC similarity.
- Read off the patch centre's location in the map, and the rotation.
- The map is [`FloorMap`](../gdnav/perception/map_matcher.py#L37) (1 cm/px, saved as `.npz`):
  [`maps/indoor_arena.npz`](../maps/indoor_arena.npz), taught with VIO + landmarks.

### 5.8 Safety

| Fault | Detection | Action | Sim injection |
|---|---|---|---|
| link | telemetry age > 1.5 s | hold | `link_loss` |
| battery | < 25 % / < 15 % | rts / land | `low_battery` |
| camera | frame age > 1 s, or the image is dark/uniform | hold if vision is needed, else warn | `camera_covered`, `camera_dropout` |
| position_quality | eph > 0.9 m | hold | `featureless_floor` |
| geofence | estimate outside the fence | rts | – |
| vehicle_failsafe | PX4 failsafe flag | warn (PX4 acts) | – |
| vio | no VIO sample for 0.5 s | warn (flow fallback) | `vio_tracking_loss` |

The rules live in [`HealthMonitor.evaluate`](../gdnav/safety/health_monitor.py#L43). The state machine reacts
in [`MissionStateMachine.step`](../gdnav/navigation/state_machine.py#L256): land > rts > hold, with
hysteresis. The injectable faults are [`FAULTS`](../gdnav/sim/sim_vehicle.py#L23), applied by
[`SimVehicle._apply_faults`](../gdnav/sim/sim_vehicle.py#L97). Response times are measured in
[`MissionRunner._event`](../gdnav/runner.py#L114).

## 10. Known limitations and risks

| Limitation | Effect | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| The simulated EKF and controller are analogues, not PX4 | Tuning may differ | PX4 SITL (L1–L3), bench logs (H10) |
| The rendered camera is geometric | Detection may drop on real or Gazebo images | Real data (H5), Gazebo frames via `record_dataset.py --gz-camera` |
| VIO numbers come from a model | Not evidence that VIO beats flow | J6 |
| The teach-and-repeat map is only as good as the teach flight | Can bias repeat flights (measured: 0.43 m before the fix) | `map_std_m`, teach with VIO + landmarks |
| The reactive planner can be trapped by concave obstacles; collision prevention covers the forward sector only | Collision risk in clutter | S1, keep the pilot in the loop |
| PX4/ROS code not yet run (blocked in the build environment) | Small fixes expected | L1–L3, J3 |
| Ultralytics is AGPL-3.0 | Licensing | S3 or an enterprise licence |

Safety rules (plan section 12) are built into [CHECKLISTS.md](CHECKLISTS.md) and into the standby
service, which never takes off on its own.
